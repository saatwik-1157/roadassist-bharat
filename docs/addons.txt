1. Edge AI & Road Hazard Engine (P3)
On-Device Pothole & Hazard Detector: Lightweight model (≤15 MB) using mitangshu11 to detect potholes, speed-breakers, and unpaved roads.
Pavement Distress Index (PDI): Live 0.0–1.0 road roughness score.
Empirical Bayes Blackspot Predictor: Uses MoRTH accident data to identify high-risk crash zones.
2. Smart Backend & Highway Mapping (P1)
Highway Milestone (LRS) Resolution: Find exact highway locations like NH-44 KM 142.5 using PostGIS LRS.
Uber H3 Hexagonal Grid (Res 8): Enables fast nearest-mechanic and responder lookups (<50 ms).
Hazard-Aware Dispatch Routing: Avoids severely damaged roads when routing tow trucks and ambulances.
3. Life-Safety & Offline Resilience (P2 & P4)
Zero-Internet Offline Client: SQLite + offline maps for fault diagnosis and roadside requests without internet.
Crash-to-Responder Engine: 100 Hz IMU crash detection with a 30-second cancellation countdown and automatic SMS location dispatch.
Feature Phone Gateway: SMS & IVR commands (MADAD, SOS, STATUS) in 8 Indian languages.
4. Government & Compliance Module (P4)
MoRTH Statutory Blackspot Portal: District heatmaps with automatic monthly statutory PDF reports.
DPDP Act 2023 Privacy Layer: Query-level k-anonymity (k ≥ 10) to prevent citizen re-identification.
